## Class Notes:

"Glue is good. But will it stay forever?"

![[anatomy of a screw.png|200]]

- Wood screws will typically have a 50%:50% Core to thread ratio.
- Machined screws typically have a 10%:1% core to thread ratio.
- Wood screws have identical threading along the entire core
- Sheet metal screws do not so the hold the sheets together
- Rivets are for when you you cannot fasten a nut from the other side
- Rivnut  is a rivet that has threading in it
